More information

Mokio Wave looks like a regular music app, but it is so much more. It introduces innovative software built specifically for you to create music directly within the app. Using the built-in editing panel, you can cut and clip your favorite tracks, then save them locally to your private library or publish them directly for the world to hear. Mokio Wave is a true all-in-one ecosystem designed for users to share, create, and earn. With just a few clicks, you will find yourself in a studio ready to build any song you can imagine.
