What’s in Mokio Messaging

More detail on the tools and features that sit behind the homepage card.

Chat

Clean, fast conversations

Direct messages and group chats with a simple layout. The goal is speed and clarity first, then extra features on top.

Play

Game integration

Messaging is the doorway to games. Play together from a conversation instead of leaving the app for a separate launcher.

Mods

Built for expansion

Mods let creators add experiences, features, and interactions on top of chat — the same path that leads into games later.

Platform

Connected to Mokio

Accounts, music, and future products stay linked. Messaging is one surface of the same platform, not a standalone silo.

More information

Mokio Arc is an open-design messaging app designed for easy communication. Arc will launch as a simple app featuring a web version connected via the Mokio app. Later on, Arc will expand into gaming with integrations that allow users to install a mod and use Mokio Arc directly within their favorite games. The platform also enables users to find and join each other's game sessions effortlessly. Additionally, Arc features seamless integration with the music app Mokio Wave, allowing users to listen to songs together with their friends.
